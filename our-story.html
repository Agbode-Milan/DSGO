Who we are

A foundation built
around one street.

Divine Selasi Global Outreach began with a simple observation — that real help works best when it's close, consistent and given with dignity.

Preparation, ahead of outreach day

Our mission

Practical support, delivered directly.

Divine Selasi Global Outreach exists to provide practical assistance and support to less privileged and vulnerable people in society, with a particular focus on Kayayes — head porters — at Agbogbloshie, Accra, especially young mothers.

We're not asking people to give into the abstract. We're connecting people who want to help with people who genuinely need support, and making sure what's given actually arrives.

Agbogbloshie, Accra

Who we serve

Kayayes, and the children who depend on them.

Kayayes are young women and girls, many of them migrants, who earn a living carrying goods on their heads through Accra's markets. Many are also mothers — raising children while working long, physically demanding hours, often with nowhere secure to sleep.

Agbogbloshie has one of the largest concentrations of Kayayes in Accra. It's where DSGO focuses its outreach, building relationships with the same community year after year rather than moving on.

Our vision

A Ghana where dignity isn't conditional on income.

We want to see a growing network of donors, volunteers and partners who treat support for vulnerable communities as normal, consistent and dependable — not a once-a-year act of charity, but an ongoing relationship.

The annual outreach

Why 26th December?

The day after Christmas is when families gather — and when the gap between those with and without is often most visible. DSGO chose it deliberately.

Before

Collection & sorting

Donations of clothing, food, stationery and essentials are collected, sorted and packed by volunteers in the weeks leading up to the outreach.

On the day

Distribution at Agbogbloshie

Volunteers travel to Agbogbloshie to distribute items directly to Kayayes and their children — in person, with conversation, not just a handout.

After

Documenting & learning

DSGO records what was given and to whom, so next year's outreach — and any support in between — can be even more effective.
