DSGO runs on people who show up — as volunteers, partners, and advocates. Here's how to start.

Volunteers, outreach day

01 — Volunteer

Join an outreach.

Volunteers help with everything from sorting and packing donations to travelling to Agbogbloshie for distribution on 26th December. No special skills are required — just time, energy, and a willingness to show up.

Tell us a little about yourself below and DSGO will reach out with the next opportunity to help.

Partnerships

02 — Partner

Partner with DSGO.

Businesses, churches, schools and other organizations can partner with DSGO through in-kind donations, corporate sponsorship, or by hosting a collection drive for the December outreach.

We'll work with your team to agree on what's needed, when, and how the partnership will be recognized.
