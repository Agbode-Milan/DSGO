Beyond the numbers

Stories, not statistics.

We tell these stories with consent, and with an emphasis on dignity, never pity.

Young Mothers

Standing with young mothers

Many of the women we meet are raising children while working long hours with very little support around them. We try to make that load a bit lighter, one outreach at a time.

Children

A childhood, looked after

Children are at the heart of every outreach, from shared meals to new clothes to simply having fun with other kids. Small comforts add up.

Community

Neighbours helping neighbours

Every outreach depends on people who choose to show up: volunteers, neighbours and local partners who give freely of their time. That generosity is what makes the work possible.
