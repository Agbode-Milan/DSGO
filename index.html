Agbogbloshie, Accra

Why we exist

We believe help should reach people where it matters most.

Kayayes — the young women and girls who carry loads for a living in Accra's markets — are some of the hardest-working, least-supported people in the city. Many are also mothers, raising children while sleeping in the open and working long hours for little pay.

Divine Selasi Global Outreach exists to close that gap: bringing together people who want to help with the people who need it, and turning that connection into clothing, food, stationery, and everyday essentials.

Our flagship outreach

Every 26th December, we show up.

Each year, DSGO brings together volunteers and donors to deliver clothing, food, stationery and personal essentials directly to Kayayes and their children at Agbogbloshie — in person, with dignity.
